Why AI Must Be Specialized & Evaluated on Financial Data

Generic large language models suffer from critical blindspots when handling legislative financial disclosures:

  • Constraint Hallucinations: Standard models fail to recognize complex parent-subsidiary structures (e.g. classifying defense contractor Huntington Ingalls as generic industrial manufacturing).
  • Severe Lookahead Bias: Off-the-shelf LLMs evaluate a 2024 historical trade using 2025/2026 price memories, generating fraudulent backtest alpha.
  • Disclosure Decay Blindness: Filings made 35+ days late have already lost their informational edge. Unspecialized models treat stale disclosures as fresh entries.
  • The Solution (JEV Layer): An independent Juried Evaluator (JEV) that audits model outputs, enforces zero-lookahead time hygiene, and vetoes constraint breaches before capital is committed.
